## Design

Three broad, rounded geometric segments form an open modular loop. Deep indigo defines the overall framework; the smaller teal segment represents an independently configurable component. Clear gaps preserve the sense of openness and connect the identity to composable harness management and continuous evolution. The mark is abstract rather than a literal letter stack, with no wordmark so it can sit alongside the project name or client name.

The palette targets deep indigo `#302A8C` and teal green `#12A28E`. This is a generated raster asset; the color values are design targets rather than an exact vector color specification.
